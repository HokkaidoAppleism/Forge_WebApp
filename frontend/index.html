About ForgeQB

Quizbowl practice with spaced repetition and adaptive difficulty.

Terms of Service

1. Acceptance of Terms

By using ForgeQB, you agree to abide by these basic Terms of Service. If you don't agree with these terms, please do not use our application.

2. Acceptable Use

We want ForgeQB to be a helpful, productive studying environment. To maintain this:

  • Appropriate Content: Do not write, upload, or create wildly inappropriate, offensive, or malicious content within your flashcards or personal notes. Keep it relevant to studying.
  • No Exploits: Do not attempt SQL injection attacks, cross-site scripting (XSS), server-side request forgery, or any other method intended to harm, scrape, or manipulate our infrastructure.

3. Enforcement & Bans

We reserve the right to monitor our systems for abuse. If you are found violating the Acceptable Use policy, specifically by attacking our servers or uploading malicious content, we will permanently ban your account and restrict your access to the service without warning.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about what is allowed, or if you encounter issues, please reach out to us at forge.quizbowl@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and software.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Analytics & Search Console: We use these to monitor website traffic, search queries, and general usage patterns. This helps us optimize our content and improve our reach.
  • User Answer Data: Within the ForgeQB application, we store data regarding your quizbowl answers, category strengths, and overall performance metrics.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Analytics: Website usage data allows us to see how visitors interact with our page, which helps us improve navigation and content.
  • Machine Learning & Adaptive Learning: The user answer data collected by the application is stored securely and will be used to train future Machine Learning models, so ForgeQB can identify and target your individual knowledge gaps.

4. Data Storage and Security

We restrict access to your personal performance data. We do not sell your data to third-party advertisers. All collected answer data is safeguarded and used strictly for internal modeling and enhancing the educational value of the application.

5. Contact the Desk

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us at forge.quizbowl@gmail.com.
